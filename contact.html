What to Include

  • Your organization or business name
  • Whether you are a business, nonprofit, or community organization

Good Starting Points

You do not need to have everything figured out before reaching out. A good starting point is simply identifying where work is getting stuck, where follow-through is being missed, or where day-to-day operations feel heavier than they should.
